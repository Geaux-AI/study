ONCE A YEAR — CHANGE THE PASSWORD

In the website folder open gate.js, change the line var PW='...', save and push. That is the only place it lives, and changing it signs out everyone still logged in — including brothers who graduated.

IF RATINGS OR UPLOADS STOP WORKING

Supabase pauses free projects that sit unused. Just opening the dashboard wakes it back up. The class pages and every study file keep working regardless — those don't depend on it.

THE BACKUP

Most study files are hosted on another chapter's site, which we don't control. A full copy lives in sigep-testbank-files on Diego's Mac. Get it onto your own machine when you take over — if that laptop dies, the copy dies with it.
